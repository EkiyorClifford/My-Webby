I’m Ekiyor, a full-stack developer in Nigeria. Self-taught and bootcamp-trained, I’ve spent the last couple of years building product surfaces end to end: the interface, the API, and the messy parts in between.

I like clear user workflows, especially product MVPs and booking/SaaS systems. When it helps the product, I’ll wire LLM features too. Day to day I ship with Next.js, and I’m comfortable in PHP/Laravel when the stack calls for it.

I care about the edges that break real users. On Serein, that meant getting multi-booking and duplicate slots right, not just shipping a pretty calendar. I tend to overdeliver past the brief when something would otherwise feel unfinished.
