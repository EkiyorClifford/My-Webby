01. Outcome / Role

Solo-built a full booking + payments + admin ops platform for a wellness spa: guests claim live slots with upfront Paystack payment; staff run walk-ins, blocked availability, services, and revenue from one dashboard.

Owned the full stack: customer booking UX, dual auth, slot engine, payment verification/webhooks, admin operations, and email notifications.

02. Problem

Spas need more than a contact form. Customers expect live availability and paid confirmation; staff need walk-ins, blocks, and revenue without double-booking the same chair at the same hour.

03. System Flow

Browse services→ Pick live slot→ Guest checkout→ Paystack→ Confirm + email

Guests book without an account. After payment, they can optionally create an account to cancel/reschedule under a 2-hour cutoff and a max of 3 reschedules.

04. What I Built

Customer

  • Service browsing + booking
  • Live slot availability (Africa/Lagos)
  • Guest checkout + upfront Paystack
  • Account auth, cancel/reschedule rules
  • Confirmation emails (Resend)

Admin

  • Allowlist-gated admin login
  • Bookings dashboard and calendar
  • Walk-ins, blocked slots, Sunday closure
  • Services CRUD (safe deactivate)
  • Paid revenue windows

05. Key Decisions

Duplicate-booking guard first. Pre-insert check plus post-insert re-check with rollback so two guests cannot claim the same hour under concurrency.

Pay before confirm. The calendar only fills with paid demand. Guest checkout removes signup friction; account creation stays optional and post-booking.

Isolated admin auth. Allowlist-gated and cookie-isolated from customer sessions.

Policy where customers look. Cancel/reschedule rules show in the UI and emails, not only in backend checks.

06. Engineering Notes

Slot engine: hours, duration, bookings, blocks, closed days, past-slot rejection

Payments: create + Paystack / verify route / HMAC webhook (idempotent confirm)

Auth: dual cookie namespaces / RLS for email-scoped booking reads

Timezone: Africa/Lagos across availability, cutoffs, and revenue windows

07. Result

A trustworthy booking loop for guests and staff: overlap-aware slots, idempotent payment confirmation, and Lagos timezone discipline. The hard part was keeping availability, payments, and operations consistent under concurrency.
