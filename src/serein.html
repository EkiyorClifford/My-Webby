01 — Outcome / Role

Solo-built a full booking + payments + admin ops platform for a wellness spa: guests claim live slots with upfront Paystack payment; staff run walk-ins, blocked availability, services, and revenue from one dashboard.

Owned the full stack — customer booking UX, dual auth, slot engine, payment verification/webhooks, admin operations, and email notifications.

02 — Problem

Spas need more than a contact form. Customers expect live availability and paid confirmation; staff need walk-ins, blocks, and revenue without double-booking the same chair at the same hour.

04 — What I Built

Customer

  • Service browsing + booking at /book/[serviceId]
  • Live slot availability (Africa/Lagos)
  • Guest checkout + upfront Paystack payment
  • Account auth, cancel/reschedule with policy rules
  • Confirmation emails (Resend)

Admin

  • Allowlist-gated admin login
  • Bookings dashboard (search / filter / calendar)
  • Walk-ins, blocked slots, Sunday closure
  • Services CRUD (safe deactivate)
  • Paid revenue: today / week / month / all-time

05 — Key Decisions

Duplicate-booking guard first. Multi-booking and overlapping slots were treated as a core product risk — pre-insert check plus post-insert re-check with rollback so two guests cannot claim the same hour under concurrency.

Pay before confirm. The calendar only fills with paid demand. Guest checkout removes signup friction; account creation stays optional and post-booking.

Isolated admin auth. Allowlist-gated and cookie-isolated from customer sessions — a role claim alone is not enough to reach operations tools.

Policy where customers look. Cancel/reschedule rules (2-hour cutoff, max 3 reschedules) show in the UI and emails, not only in backend checks.

06 — Engineering Notes

Slot engine → hours, duration, bookings, blocks, closed days, past-slot rejection

Payments → create + Paystack initiate · verify route · HMAC webhook (idempotent confirm)

Auth → dual cookie namespaces (customer vs admin) · RLS for email-scoped booking reads

Timezone → Africa/Lagos across availability, cutoffs, and revenue windows

Verify route and webhook share confirmation logic so retries and race conditions do not double-confirm a booking.

07 — Result

A trustworthy booking loop for guests and staff: overlap-aware slots, idempotent payment confirmation, and Lagos timezone discipline. The hard part was not the form — it was keeping availability, payments, and operations consistent under concurrency.
